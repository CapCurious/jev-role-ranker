Title: Warehouse Operations Lead
Company: Pennywhistle Schools
Location: Denver, CO

At Pennywhistle Schools, teachers are our first customers and our first critics. This Warehouse Operations Lead position reports to a director and works with several teams. You will work with colleagues in several departments to keep projects moving. You will track a handful of numbers that tell the team how the work is going. You will improve the processes your team uses and document what you change. You bring 4 years of experience and have shipped work end to end. Graduate business training is a plus. This role is fully onsite at our Denver office. Only permanent residents and citizens may apply. The interview process has three short conversations.
