Title: Senior Technical Product Manager
Company: Northwind Robotics
Location: Remote (United States)

At Northwind Robotics, small teams ship hardware and software together on a weekly rhythm. We are looking for a Senior Technical Product Manager to join a team that is growing this year. You will run launches from first draft to the first month of usage data. You will define success metrics and review them with engineering and design. You will interview customers every week and turn what you hear into priorities. You have 8 or more years of experience and have led a team before. An MBA or equivalent graduate degree is welcome but not required. This is a fully remote role for candidates based in the United States. Our benefits include health coverage and a learning budget. We review applications on a rolling basis.
