Title: Associate Product Manager, Platform
Company: Brightmoor Cloud
Location: New York, NY

Brightmoor Cloud offers a data platform that small companies use to understand their customers. This Associate Product Manager, Platform position reports to a director and works with several teams. You will run launches from first draft to the first month of usage data. You will define success metrics and review them with engineering and design. You will set the roadmap, write clear specs and decide what ships next. You have 0 to 2 years of relevant experience, and recent graduates are welcome. You are comfortable with data pipelines, dashboards and experiments. We hire MBA graduates for this role every year. This role is fully onsite at our New York office. Our benefits include health coverage and a learning budget.
