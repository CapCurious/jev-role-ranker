Title: Group Operations Manager
Company: Brightmoor Cloud
Location: Chicago, IL

Brightmoor Cloud sells workflow software to teams that outgrew their spreadsheets. This Group Operations Manager position reports to a director and works with several teams. You will work with colleagues in several departments to keep projects moving. You will improve the processes your team uses and document what you change. You bring 10 years of experience and a record of mentoring others. A technical background is expected, and you can read code or write simple queries. The team works from our Chicago office three days a week and remotely the rest. We welcome applicants from every background. Send us a short note about a project you are proud of.
