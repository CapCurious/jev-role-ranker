Title: Product Manager, Data
Company: Maplegate Markets
Location: Austin, TX

At Maplegate Markets, a small team looks after the whole path from search to delivery. This Product Manager, Data position reports to a director and works with several teams. You will weigh tradeoffs between scope, cost and timing and explain your choices. You will define success metrics and review them with engineering and design. You will set the roadmap, write clear specs and decide what ships next. You have up to 2 years of experience, and we expect to teach you the rest. A technical background is expected, and you can read code or write simple queries. This role is fully onsite at our Austin office. Applicants must be authorized to work in the United States without current or future sponsorship. The interview process has three short conversations.
