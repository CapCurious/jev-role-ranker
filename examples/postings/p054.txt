Title: Data Analyst
Company: Cragmoor Energy
Location: Austin, TX

At Cragmoor Energy, the work is practical and the customers are cities, schools and factories. We are looking for a Data Analyst to join a team that is growing this year. You will track a handful of numbers that tell the team how the work is going. You will prepare summaries for leadership and flag risks early. You have 3 to 5 years of experience in a similar role. You will work daily with engineers on APIs, data models and system tradeoffs. We hire MBA graduates for this role every year. This role is fully onsite at our Austin office. Visa sponsorship is available for qualified candidates. Send us a short note about a project you are proud of.
