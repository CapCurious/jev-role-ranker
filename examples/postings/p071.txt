Title: Operations Manager
Company: Redwood Freight
Location: Remote (Canada)

Redwood Freight moves freight for regional retailers and tracks every shipment end to end. This Operations Manager position reports to a director and works with several teams. You will work with colleagues in several departments to keep projects moving. You will track a handful of numbers that tell the team how the work is going. You bring 4 years of experience and have shipped work end to end. An MBA or equivalent graduate degree is welcome but not required. This is a fully remote role for candidates based in Canada. Visa sponsorship is available for qualified candidates. The interview process has three short conversations.
