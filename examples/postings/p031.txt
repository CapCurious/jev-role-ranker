Title: Associate Product Manager, Platform
Company: Hollowell Goods
Location: Boston, MA

Hollowell Goods runs an online marketplace for household goods from independent makers. We are looking for a Associate Product Manager, Platform to join a team that is growing this year. You will weigh tradeoffs between scope, cost and timing and explain your choices. You will run launches from first draft to the first month of usage data. You have 0 to 2 years of relevant experience, and recent graduates are welcome. A technical background is expected, and you can read code or write simple queries. An MBA or equivalent graduate degree is welcome but not required. The team works from our Boston office three days a week and remotely the rest. Send us a short note about a project you are proud of.
