Title: Group Operations Manager
Company: Copperline Automation
Location: Bengaluru, India

You have 8 or more years of experience and have led a team before. This role is fully onsite at our Bengaluru office. Visa sponsorship is available for qualified candidates. We review applications on a rolling basis.
