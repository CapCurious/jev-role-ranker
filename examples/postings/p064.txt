Title: Financial Analyst
Company: Solstice Grid
Location: Remote (United Kingdom)

An MBA or equivalent graduate degree is welcome but not required. This is a fully remote role for candidates based in United Kingdom. We welcome applicants from every background. Send us a short note about a project you are proud of.
