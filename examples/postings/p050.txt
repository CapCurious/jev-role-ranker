Title: Senior Technical Product Manager
Company: Oakhaven Lending
Location: Raleigh, NC

At Oakhaven Lending, careful engineering and clear communication matter more than speed. We are hiring for the Senior Technical Product Manager role on a team of about twelve people. You will run launches from first draft to the first month of usage data. You will define success metrics and review them with engineering and design. You will interview customers every week and turn what you hear into priorities. You have 8 or more years of experience and have led a team before. A technical background is expected, and you can read code or write simple queries. An MBA or equivalent graduate degree is welcome but not required. The team works from our Raleigh office three days a week and remotely the rest. We welcome applicants from every background.
