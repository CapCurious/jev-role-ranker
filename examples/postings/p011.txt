Title: Product Manager, Growth
Company: Northwind Robotics
Location: New York, NY

Northwind Robotics builds robots that move boxes, scan shelves and inspect equipment in busy facilities. We are looking for a Product Manager, Growth to join a team that is growing this year. You will weigh tradeoffs between scope, cost and timing and explain your choices. You will interview customers every week and turn what you hear into priorities. You will define success metrics and review them with engineering and design. You have 3 to 5 years of experience in a similar role. Graduate business training is a plus. This role is fully onsite at our New York office. US citizenship is required for this role. The interview process has three short conversations.
