Title: Field Marketing Manager
Company: Fernhill Data
Location: Denver, CO

At Fernhill Data, we ship to customers every week and talk to them even more often. We are hiring for the Field Marketing Manager role on a team of about twelve people. You will track a handful of numbers that tell the team how the work is going. You will own a set of recurring tasks and report on progress each month. You have 0 to 2 years of relevant experience, and recent graduates are welcome. The team works from our Denver office three days a week and remotely the rest. The interview process has three short conversations.
