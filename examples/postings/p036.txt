Title: Principal Product Manager, Growth
Company: Pinecrest Diagnostics
Location: Chicago, IL

Pinecrest Diagnostics makes tools that connect patients with the right clinician faster. This Principal Product Manager, Growth position reports to a director and works with several teams. You will define success metrics and review them with engineering and design. You will weigh tradeoffs between scope, cost and timing and explain your choices. You will interview customers every week and turn what you hear into priorities. You have 8 or more years of experience and have led a team before. You are comfortable with data pipelines, dashboards and experiments. The team works from our Chicago office three days a week and remotely the rest. Our benefits include health coverage and a learning budget. We review applications on a rolling basis.
