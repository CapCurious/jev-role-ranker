Title: Technical Product Manager
Company: Anchorpoint Supply
Location: Bengaluru, India

At Anchorpoint Supply, thousands of deliveries a day depend on the systems this team owns. This Technical Product Manager position reports to a director and works with several teams. You will define success metrics and review them with engineering and design. You will weigh tradeoffs between scope, cost and timing and explain your choices. You have 3 to 5 years of experience in a similar role. You will work daily with engineers on APIs, data models and system tradeoffs. We hire MBA graduates for this role every year. This role is fully onsite at our Bengaluru office. Our benefits include health coverage and a learning budget. The interview process has three short conversations.
