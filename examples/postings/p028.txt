Title: Product Manager, Platform
Company: Copperline Automation
Location: Remote (United States)

Copperline Automation builds robots that move boxes, scan shelves and inspect equipment in busy facilities. This Product Manager, Platform position reports to a director and works with several teams. You will set the roadmap, write clear specs and decide what ships next. You will interview customers every week and turn what you hear into priorities. You will define success metrics and review them with engineering and design. You have 3 to 5 years of experience in a similar role. A technical background is expected, and you can read code or write simple queries. An MBA or equivalent graduate degree is welcome but not required. This is a fully remote role for candidates based in the United States. We have supported work visas for new hires before and are glad to do it again.
