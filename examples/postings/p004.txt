Title: Associate Product Manager, Platform
Company: Solstice Grid
Location: Atlanta, GA

Solstice Grid installs and monitors storage systems for commercial buildings. We are looking for a Associate Product Manager, Platform to join a team that is growing this year. You will set the roadmap, write clear specs and decide what ships next. You will weigh tradeoffs between scope, cost and timing and explain your choices. You will interview customers every week and turn what you hear into priorities. You have 0 to 2 years of relevant experience, and recent graduates are welcome. An MBA or equivalent graduate degree is welcome but not required. This role is fully onsite at our Atlanta office. The interview process has three short conversations.
