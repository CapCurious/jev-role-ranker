Title: Principal Financial Analyst
Company: Larkspur Academy
Location: New York, NY

At Larkspur Academy, teachers are our first customers and our first critics. We are hiring for the Principal Financial Analyst role on a team of about twelve people. You will track a handful of numbers that tell the team how the work is going. You will prepare summaries for leadership and flag risks early. You will manage relationships with customers and partners over the long term. You have 8 or more years of experience and have led a team before. You will work daily with engineers on APIs, data models and system tradeoffs. The team works from our New York office three days a week and remotely the rest. US citizenship is required for this role. The interview process has three short conversations.
