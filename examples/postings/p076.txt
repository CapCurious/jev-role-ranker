Title: Warehouse Operations Lead
Company: Stonebridge Shipping
Location: Bengaluru, India

Stonebridge Shipping runs a network of depots and software that plans the routes between them. This Warehouse Operations Lead position reports to a director and works with several teams. You will manage relationships with customers and partners over the long term. You will improve the processes your team uses and document what you change. You have 3 to 5 years of experience in a similar role. This role is fully onsite at our Bengaluru office. Only permanent residents and citizens may apply. Send us a short note about a project you are proud of.
