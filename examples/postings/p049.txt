Title: Senior Product Manager
Company: Vesperlight Health
Location: Raleigh, NC

Vesperlight Health makes tools that connect patients with the right clinician faster. We are hiring for the Senior Product Manager role on a team of about twelve people. You will set the roadmap, write clear specs and decide what ships next. You will run launches from first draft to the first month of usage data. You will interview customers every week and turn what you hear into priorities. You have 8 or more years of experience and have led a team before. An MBA or equivalent graduate degree is welcome but not required. This role is fully onsite at our Raleigh office. We review applications on a rolling basis.
