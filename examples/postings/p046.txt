Title: Product Manager, Data
Company: Tessel Machines
Location: New York, NY

At Tessel Machines, small teams ship hardware and software together on a weekly rhythm. We are looking for a Product Manager, Data to join a team that is growing this year. You will interview customers every week and turn what you hear into priorities. You will weigh tradeoffs between scope, cost and timing and explain your choices. You have 3 to 5 years of experience in a similar role. A technical background is expected, and you can read code or write simple queries. This role is fully onsite at our New York office. This position does not offer visa sponsorship of any kind. We review applications on a rolling basis.
