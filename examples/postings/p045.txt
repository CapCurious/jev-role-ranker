Title: Product Manager, Data
Company: Fernhill Data
Location: Chicago, IL

At Fernhill Data, we ship to customers every week and talk to them even more often. We are hiring for the Product Manager, Data role on a team of about twelve people. You will interview customers every week and turn what you hear into priorities. You will run launches from first draft to the first month of usage data. You have 3 to 5 years of experience in a similar role. You will work daily with engineers on APIs, data models and system tradeoffs. This role is fully onsite at our Chicago office. Applicants must be authorized to work in the United States without current or future sponsorship. We review applications on a rolling basis.
