Title: Senior Product Manager
Company: Larkspur Academy
Location: Seattle, WA

Larkspur Academy offers tutoring and course tools to schools that want better outcomes. This Senior Product Manager position reports to a director and works with several teams. You will run launches from first draft to the first month of usage data. You will weigh tradeoffs between scope, cost and timing and explain your choices. You bring 10 years of experience and a record of mentoring others. You are comfortable with data pipelines, dashboards and experiments. The team works from our Seattle office three days a week and remotely the rest. Applicants must be authorized to work in the United States without current or future sponsorship. The interview process has three short conversations.
