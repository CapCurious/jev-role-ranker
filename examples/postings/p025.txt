Title: Associate Data Analyst
Company: Keelhaven Freightworks
Location: Remote (United States)

Keelhaven Freightworks moves freight for regional retailers and tracks every shipment end to end. We are looking for a Associate Data Analyst to join a team that is growing this year. You will track a handful of numbers that tell the team how the work is going. You will improve the processes your team uses and document what you change. You will work with colleagues in several departments to keep projects moving. You have 0 to 2 years of relevant experience, and recent graduates are welcome. A technical background is expected, and you can read code or write simple queries. An MBA or equivalent graduate degree is welcome but not required. This is a fully remote role for candidates based in the United States. This position does not offer visa sponsorship of any kind.
