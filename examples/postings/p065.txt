Title: Associate Field Marketing Manager
Company: Oakhaven Lending
Location: Boston, MA

At Oakhaven Lending, careful engineering and clear communication matter more than speed. This Associate Field Marketing Manager position reports to a director and works with several teams. You will improve the processes your team uses and document what you change. You will own a set of recurring tasks and report on progress each month. You will track a handful of numbers that tell the team how the work is going. You have 0 to 2 years of relevant experience, and recent graduates are welcome. An MBA or equivalent graduate degree is welcome but not required. The team works from our Boston office three days a week and remotely the rest. We have supported work visas for new hires before and are glad to do it again. We review applications on a rolling basis.
