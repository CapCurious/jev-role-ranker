Title: Principal Product Manager
Company: Copperline Automation
Location: Raleigh, NC

At Copperline Automation, small teams ship hardware and software together on a weekly rhythm. We are hiring for the Principal Product Manager role on a team of about twelve people. You will interview customers every week and turn what you hear into priorities. You will set the roadmap, write clear specs and decide what ships next. You bring 10 years of experience and a record of mentoring others. A technical background is expected, and you can read code or write simple queries. An MBA or equivalent graduate degree is welcome but not required. This role is fully onsite at our Raleigh office. We review applications on a rolling basis.
