Title: Group Product Manager, Growth
Company: Pennywhistle Schools
Location: Remote (United States)

At Pennywhistle Schools, teachers are our first customers and our first critics. We are looking for a Group Product Manager, Growth to join a team that is growing this year. You will set the roadmap, write clear specs and decide what ships next. You will weigh tradeoffs between scope, cost and timing and explain your choices. You will interview customers every week and turn what you hear into priorities. You bring 10 years of experience and a record of mentoring others. We hire MBA graduates for this role every year. This is a fully remote role for candidates based in the United States. The interview process has three short conversations.
