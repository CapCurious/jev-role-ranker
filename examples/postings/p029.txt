Title: Account Executive
Company: Copperline Automation
Location: Raleigh, NC

Copperline Automation designs warehouse automation used by mid-sized operators across the country. We are hiring for the Account Executive role on a team of about twelve people. You will manage relationships with customers and partners over the long term. You will improve the processes your team uses and document what you change. You will track a handful of numbers that tell the team how the work is going. You have 3 to 5 years of experience in a similar role. The team works from our Raleigh office three days a week and remotely the rest. We have supported work visas for new hires before and are glad to do it again. We review applications on a rolling basis.
