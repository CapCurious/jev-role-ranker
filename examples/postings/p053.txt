Title: Group Technical Product Manager
Company: Maplegate Markets
Location: Remote (United States)

Maplegate Markets runs an online marketplace for household goods from independent makers. We are hiring for the Group Technical Product Manager role on a team of about twelve people. You will run launches from first draft to the first month of usage data. You will interview customers every week and turn what you hear into priorities. You will define success metrics and review them with engineering and design. You bring 10 years of experience and a record of mentoring others. A technical background is expected, and you can read code or write simple queries. This is a fully remote role for candidates based in the United States. The interview process has three short conversations.
