Title: Product Manager, Platform
Company: Redwood Freight
Location: Remote (United States)

Redwood Freight runs a network of depots and software that plans the routes between them. We are looking for a Product Manager, Platform to join a team that is growing this year. You will weigh tradeoffs between scope, cost and timing and explain your choices. You will set the roadmap, write clear specs and decide what ships next. You have 3 to 5 years of experience in a similar role. A technical background is expected, and you can read code or write simple queries. We hire MBA graduates for this role every year. This is a fully remote role for candidates based in the United States. We review applications on a rolling basis.
