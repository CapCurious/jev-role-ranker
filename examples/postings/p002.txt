Title: Operations Manager
Company: Larkspur Academy
Location: Raleigh, NC

Larkspur Academy builds learning software used in classrooms every day. We are hiring for the Operations Manager role on a team of about twelve people. You will work with colleagues in several departments to keep projects moving. You will improve the processes your team uses and document what you change. You will track a handful of numbers that tell the team how the work is going. You bring 4 years of experience and have shipped work end to end. The team works from our Raleigh office three days a week and remotely the rest. We review applications on a rolling basis.
