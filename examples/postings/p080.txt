Title: Senior Customer Success Manager
Company: Cragmoor Energy
Location: Remote (United States)

At Cragmoor Energy, the work is practical and the customers are cities, schools and factories. We are looking for a Senior Customer Success Manager to join a team that is growing this year. You will prepare summaries for leadership and flag risks early. You will track a handful of numbers that tell the team how the work is going. You will work with colleagues in several departments to keep projects moving. You have 8 or more years of experience and have led a team before. Graduate business training is a plus. This is a fully remote role for candidates based in the United States. Our benefits include health coverage and a learning budget. The interview process has three short conversations.
