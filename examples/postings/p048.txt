Title: Technical Product Manager
Company: Solstice Grid
Location: Chicago, IL

Solstice Grid installs and monitors storage systems for commercial buildings. We are looking for a Technical Product Manager to join a team that is growing this year. You will set the roadmap, write clear specs and decide what ships next. You will weigh tradeoffs between scope, cost and timing and explain your choices. You have 3 to 5 years of experience in a similar role. A technical background is expected, and you can read code or write simple queries. We hire MBA graduates for this role every year. This role is fully onsite at our Chicago office. We welcome applicants from every background. Send us a short note about a project you are proud of.
