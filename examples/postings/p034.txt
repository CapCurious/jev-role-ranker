Title: Account Executive
Company: Stonebridge Shipping
Location: Remote (Ireland)

You bring 4 years of experience and have shipped work end to end. Graduate business training is a plus. This is a fully remote role for candidates based in Ireland. We have supported work visas for new hires before and are glad to do it again. The interview process has three short conversations.
