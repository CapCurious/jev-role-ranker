Title: Associate Product Manager, Growth
Company: Tessel Machines
Location: Chicago, IL

Tessel Machines designs warehouse automation used by mid-sized operators across the country. We are hiring for the Associate Product Manager, Growth role on a team of about twelve people. You will interview customers every week and turn what you hear into priorities. You will run launches from first draft to the first month of usage data. You have up to 2 years of experience, and we expect to teach you the rest. A technical background is expected, and you can read code or write simple queries. This role is fully onsite at our Chicago office. We welcome applicants from every background. We review applications on a rolling basis.
