Title: Product Manager, Data
Company: Keelhaven Freightworks
Location: Boston, MA

Keelhaven Freightworks runs a network of depots and software that plans the routes between them. This Product Manager, Data position reports to a director and works with several teams. You will interview customers every week and turn what you hear into priorities. You will set the roadmap, write clear specs and decide what ships next. You have 3 to 5 years of experience in a similar role. The team works from our Boston office three days a week and remotely the rest. Visa sponsorship is available for qualified candidates. The interview process has three short conversations.
