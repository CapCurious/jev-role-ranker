Title: Data Analyst
Company: Maplegate Markets
Location: San Francisco, CA

Maplegate Markets runs an online marketplace for household goods from independent makers. This Data Analyst position reports to a director and works with several teams. You will manage relationships with customers and partners over the long term. You will track a handful of numbers that tell the team how the work is going. You will own a set of recurring tasks and report on progress each month. You bring 4 years of experience and have shipped work end to end. You will work daily with engineers on APIs, data models and system tradeoffs. The team works from our San Francisco office three days a week and remotely the rest. We are unable to sponsor work visas for this position. The interview process has three short conversations.
