Title: Principal Field Marketing Manager
Company: Keelhaven Freightworks
Location: Remote (Canada)

Keelhaven Freightworks moves freight for regional retailers and tracks every shipment end to end. We are hiring for the Principal Field Marketing Manager role on a team of about twelve people. You will track a handful of numbers that tell the team how the work is going. You will prepare summaries for leadership and flag risks early. You will manage relationships with customers and partners over the long term. You have 8 or more years of experience and have led a team before. We hire MBA graduates for this role every year. This is a fully remote role for candidates based in Canada. The interview process has three short conversations.
